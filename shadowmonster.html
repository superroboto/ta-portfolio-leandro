Shadow Monster: Dynamic 2D Projection and Traversal

A 3D character rendered as a 2D shadow entity with a dynamic wall-navigation system. It can navigate any wall that is large enough. If players venture too close, the monster transitions its limbs into 3D space to kill the player.

2D Shadow Projection

The system utilizes an orthographic SceneCaptureComponent2D that renders only the monster's skeletal mesh and a custom Niagara trail system.

This Render Target Texture is then projected onto the environment using a decal material.

2D to 3D Transition

To achieve the horror effect where the creature reaches out of the wall into 3D space:

  • The system maintains a synchronized 3D duplicate skeletal mesh.
  • A custom material mask keeps the 3D geometry completely invisible behind the wall's surface plane.
  • As limbs break through the plane threshold, they become visible in 3D space, creating a seamless 2D-to-3D transition.

Wall Traversal

A custom wall traversal system allows this monster to be spawned on any flat wall and move along it's length, trying its best to reach nearby players.

It can jump across gaps, jump onto/over obstacles, and supports uneven terrain. This is done using traces which are done every 0.05sec, unlike the movement itself which updates on tick to remain perfectly smooth.

Optimization & Performance

When standing directly in front of the monster, the average render target capture time at 60 FPS is 0.05ms (0.15ms every 3 frames) tested on a Radeon RX 5700.

Because SceneCapture2D components can quickly become performance bottlenecks in Unreal Engine, this feature was built with aggressive optimization:

Stripped Rendering

The Scene Capture is limited to BaseColor and Opacity, bypassing expensive lighting, shadow casting, and post-processing passes.

Stylized Stop-Motion

The capture rate is limited to 20 FPS. This reduces CPU/GPU capture overhead while intentionally introducing a creepy feel that enhances the monster's otherworldly movement.

Distance-Based Resolution Scaling

The capture resolution scales down as the player moves further away, heavily reducing SceneCapture2D cost on distant entities.

Frustum & Occlusion Culling

When the creature is outside the player's view frustum or completely occluded behind geometry:

  • Render capture is entirely frozen to eliminate unnecessary overhead.
  • Movement logic continues to evaluate in the background.
